# 🛒 Shopease

Shopease is an online grocery and vegetable shopping website designed to make everyday grocery shopping simple and convenient.

The platform allows users to browse available vegetables and grocery products, upload/add products, add items to their cart, and purchase them online. It provides a user-friendly shopping experience with product management and billing functionality.

### Key Features

* 🥦 Vegetable & grocery product listing
* ➕ Add/upload products
* 🛒 Add products to cart
* 💳 Shopping and billing functionality
* 🧾 Bill/receipt generation
* 📱 User-friendly and responsive interface

**Shopease – Making everyday grocery shopping easier, faster, and more convenient.**
